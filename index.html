About Studio Motion

Studio Motion is a hobby project, not made by fitness specialists. The illustrations are simplified cues, not professional training or medical guidance. Follow your equipment’s instructions.

Workouts and checkmarks stay in this browser. There is no account, tracking, or workout sync. The online app and a downloaded copy do not share saved plans.
