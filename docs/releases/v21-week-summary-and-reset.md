# v21 · Week summary and local data controls

The current workout shows its muscle groups. **Week summary** is a read-only view of seven dated workouts, their exercise counts, and muscle groups, including date-specific replacements. Pick a day to jump to it. Opening the summary or changing the displayed day does not modify the workout.

Menu includes a local **Reset Studio Motion** action on browsers that support exclusive reset coordination. It takes a typed confirmation followed by the explicit reset button. It clears this browser copy's workout plans, archived sessions, dated checkmarks and replacements, and preferences. It does not remove the cached offline app or other local data. Close older Studio Motion tabs before resetting. Two up-to-date tabs cannot reset at the same time; after an interruption, the app requires a fresh confirmation. On a browser without safe coordination, the reset is refused and no data is deleted.

Verified in Chromium phone emulation at 320, 390 and 412 px, in English and Brazilian Portuguese: seven dated entries, empty/rest and date-specific triceps groups, correct jump and keyboard focus, unchanged storage during summary browsing, typed confirmation and explicit click, Cancel and Escape, exact-scoped synthetic cleanup, and stale v21 tabs updating without restoring data. Independent tests held the shared lock to verify busy handling, recovered an interrupted reset only after fresh consent, and forced unavailable coordination to verify refusal. An offline install retained its app cache after reset. The 10 mobile workflows and all 108 enlarged-text screen checks also passed. No actual workout data was reset in verification or deployment.
