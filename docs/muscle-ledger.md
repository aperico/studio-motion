# Studio Motion v37 per-exercise ledger

Decisions are from the three independent audit batches. Retained means the default remains reasonable. Schematic flags a pose where the simplified rig does not establish exact joint angle, grip, or rotation. Adjusted entries include target, cue, or illustration changes. A study link denotes directly relevant evidence and does not establish every supporting muscle or validate the drawing. Other entries use the movement mechanics plus the instructional family references below.

- Press, pull, shoulder and arm families use [ACE exercise instructions](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/body-part/arms/) and [ACE shoulder instructions](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/body-part/shoulders/).
- Knee, hip, and calf families use exercise mechanics and [ACE leg instructions](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/body-part/legs-thighs/). Narrow findings, including calf posture, do not set a default growth ranking. [Calf study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38156065/)
- Trunk and mobility entries distinguish loaded movement, bracing, movement control, stretching and breathing. [ACE core instructions](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/body-part/abs/)
